# 7 Smart Ways to Enjoy Chocolate Without Blowing Your Calories for the Day

There are plenty of smart ways to enjoy chocolate without blowing your calories for the day. I love chocolate and it is a huge part of my life, especially during that week and a half of the month when cravings hit hardest. Most women are totally going to get that. Figuring out how to satisfy a rich craving without blowing your diet can feel challenging, but over time I have come up with some reliable strategies that I am excited to share with you.

| Low-Calorie Treat | Approx. Calories | Key Highlight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot Chocolate | 90 kcal | Warm, comforting, and customizable with skim milk or water |
| Fat-Free Pudding | 90–100 kcal | Silky texture that feels like a decadent dessert |
| Fudgesicles | 60–80 kcal | Built-in portion control on a stick |

## 1. Hot Chocolate

Hot chocolate is one of the most wonderful ways to enjoy chocolate without blowing your calories for the day. While traditional cafe beverages can quickly become high-calorie treats—especially if you load them up with whipped cream and sugary syrups—you can easily make a light version at home. Preparing a hot cocoa mix with hot water keeps it right around 90 calories per mug. If you choose a diet or light hot cocoa blend, you can cut the calorie count even further while still getting that soothing cocoa fix. For another chilled option, see [chocolate milk](#6) below.

- Mix with hot water or unsweetened almond milk for a lighter base.

- Skip heavy whipped cream and dust with a sprinkle of cinnamon or nutmeg instead.

## 2. Fat Free Chocolate Pudding

I love chocolate pudding, so it is absolutely no sacrifice for me to enjoy chocolate this way. Opting for fat-free pudding shaves off extra calories and fat from an already light snack. In fact, fat-free versions often have an exceptionally smooth texture. If you want to make it feel extra indulgent, chill your pudding cups thoroughly in the refrigerator or freeze them briefly before eating to create a thick, velvety dessert experience.

## 3. Fudgesicles

Who doesn’t love these classic fudge pops? They offer a rich, icy chocolate flavor while staying remarkably low in calories. For many of us, the only real challenge is portion control because they are so delicious. Stick to a single serving size, and you can satisfy your sweet tooth without disrupting your nutritional goals for the day.

## 4. Have Dark Chocolate Instead

Believe it or not, dark chocolate with higher cocoa percentages often contains less sugar and fewer overall calories than traditional milk chocolate. While dark chocolate has a bolder, slightly bitter profile, it is incredibly satisfying. When nothing short of real chocolate candy will do, reach for a square of dark chocolate over milk chocolate. A high-quality option like [Ghirardelli](https://www.ghirardelli.com) intense dark chocolate delivers deep cocoa flavor in small, satisfying portions.

- Look for dark chocolate with 70% cocoa or higher for maximum antioxidants.

- Savor one square slowly to let the rich cocoa melt instead of chewing it quickly.

## 5. Melt 3 Hershey’s Kisses or Dove Promises

Melting a few individual chocolates is a clever trick to control your portion size while enjoying the real thing. Taking two or three [Hershey’s](https://www.hersheyland.com) Kisses or [Dove](https://www.dovechocolate.com) Promises and gently warming them creates a rich, warm dip. Dipping fresh strawberries or banana slices into the melted chocolate satisfies your craving while adding valuable fiber and fruit to your daily nutrition, much like pairing fruit with [fat free chocolate pudding](#2).

## 6. Chocolate Milk

A cold glass of chocolate milk instantly brings back childhood nostalgia. You do not need heavy syrup to get great flavor; stirring just one level teaspoon of cocoa powder into eight ounces of skim or fat-free milk keeps calories minimal. Plus, this option provides a helpful boost of calcium and protein to support your daily wellness goals, similar to enjoying warm [hot chocolate](#1) on a cold afternoon.

## 7. Tootsie Rolls

[Tootsie Rolls](https://tootsie.com) offer a fun, chewy alternative to traditional chocolate bars. Because of their dense, chewy texture, they take longer to eat, allowing you to savor the chocolate flavor far longer than a standard piece of candy. Since they are naturally lower in fat, having a mini or half-size portion easily satisfies sweet cravings without adding excess calories to your day.

Chocolate is something I simply cannot eliminate from my diet, but finding smart, low-calorie ways to enjoy it makes all the difference. How do you like to enjoy chocolate without blowing your calories for the day? I would love to hear your favorite tips!
